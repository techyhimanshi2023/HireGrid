* **Category:** Business / Productivity
* **Tags:** Job Search, Recruitment, Hiring, Careers, ATS Resume Analyzer
* **Short Description (max 80 chars):**
  ```text
  Smart hiring and AI-powered recruitment platform.
  ```
* **Full Description (max 4000 chars):**
  ```text
  HireGrid connects job seekers and recruiters seamlessly.

  Key Features:
  • Explore verified job vacancies with instant search and filters (Remote, Hybrid, Onsite).
  • 5-Stage Audited Hiring Workflow: Track applications in real-time through Applied, Under Review, Shortlisted, Interview, and Selected.
  • Built-In ATS Compatibility Engine: Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX) to get a compatibility score, skill extraction, strengths, weaknesses, and actionable recommendations.
  • Dual-Role Platform: Dedicated dashboards for both Candidates and Employers/Recruiters.
  • Secure & Private: Strict data privacy with end-to-end encrypted transit and full in-app account deletion control.
  ```

## 5. Google Play Data Safety Answers

When completing the **Data Safety** questionnaire in Google Play Console:

* **Does your app collect or share any user data?** $\rightarrow$ **Yes**
* **Is all of the user data collected encrypted in transit?** $\rightarrow$ **Yes** (HTTPS/TLS)
* **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?** $\rightarrow$ **Yes** (In-app Profile deletion)
* **Data Types Collected:**
  - **Personal info:**
    - *Name* (App functionality, Account management)
    - *Email address* (Account management, Authentication)
  - **Files and docs:**
    - *Files and docs (Resumes/CVs)* (App functionality - Job applications & ATS analysis)
  - **Device or other IDs:**
    - *User ID* (Account management)
* **Is any data shared with third-party companies or organizations?** $\rightarrow$ **No** (Zero third-party sharing)
* **Are user data used for advertising or marketing?** $\rightarrow$ **No**
